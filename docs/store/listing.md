## Short description (Play, 80 characters)

```
Review merge requests, watch pipelines and read job logs on your GitLab.
```

## Full description

```
Review a merge request on the train. Check why the pipeline went red before you
get to your desk. LabFox brings the parts of GitLab you touch every day onto
your phone, without asking you to pinch and scroll through the web UI.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Read merge requests with descriptions, labels, approvals and discussions
• Review diffs file by file with line numbers and changed lines highlighted
• Approve, merge, or leave a comment
• Watch pipelines and read full job logs with runner colours intact
• Retry or cancel jobs, and run manual jobs
• Work through issues, your to-do inbox, and project search
• Browse repositories, branches, commits, and files
• Connect multiple GitLab accounts, including self-hosted instances
• Choose background to-do checks and keep as many favorites as you need

All LabFox features are free on Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS. There are no
ads or in-app purchases. Your GitLab instance still controls permissions and
features available under its own plan.

The layout follows the screen: a focused single pane on phones and more room
on tablets and desktop. Sign in to gitlab.com or your company's instance with
a personal access token or, where supported, OAuth.

Tokens stay in your device's secure storage. There is no LabFox account or
LabFox server between the app and your GitLab instance. Anonymous product
analytics are described in the privacy policy.

LabFox is open source under Apache-2.0:
github.com/theorvane/labfox

Privacy Policy: https://www.sloki9637.com/privacy

LabFox is an unofficial, third-party client. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or certified by GitLab Inc. "GitLab" is a trademark of GitLab Inc.
```
